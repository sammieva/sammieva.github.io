顧問與產學合作Advisory & Industry Partnerships

擔任委員，參與國立臺南大學性別培力研究中心承接教育部國教署之 115 年度幼教指引計畫，協助研擬教保服務機構因應生對生疑似性別事件之防治指引手冊。Serving as a committee member on the 2026 guidelines project, commissioned by Taiwan's K-12 Education Administration and led by the Gender Equity Empowerment Research Center at National University of Tainan, helping develop a prevention-guidelines handbook for childcare institutions responding to suspected peer-to-peer gender incidents.
