研究Research

腦科學研究 × 教學實踐Brain Science Research × Teaching Practice

我的研究工作分為兩條互補的路徑——一條是探討互動關係與大腦發展的發展神經科學研究，另一條是紮根於師資培育現場的教學實踐研究。 My work runs along two complementary tracks — developmental neuroscience research on interaction and brain development, and teaching-practice research grounded in teacher education.

研究方向一Research Track 1

發展神經科學：親子／師生互動 × 執行功能 × 大腦Developmental Neuroscience: Parent-Child／Teacher-Student Interaction × Executive Function × Brain

親子互動與幼兒發展Parent-Child Interaction & Child Development 師生互動與大腦發展Teacher-Student Interaction & Brain Development 認知發展Cognitive Development 親職教育Parenting Education fNIRSfNIRS

在博士攻讀期間，我加入王馨敏教授創立的「嬰幼兒大腦發展與學習實驗室」，展開一系列結合認知發展與神經科學的研究。我聚焦於早期親子互動品質與執行功能發展之關聯性，並運用自然情境下的行為觀察與功能性近紅外光譜（fNIRS）技術，探索嬰兒期正向父母行為對兒童前額葉活化的潛在影響。

During my doctoral studies, I joined the Infant Brain Development and Learning Lab founded by Professor Hsin-Min Wang, pursuing a research program that bridges cognitive development and neuroscience. My work focuses on the relationship between early parent-child interaction quality and executive function development, combining naturalistic behavioral observation with functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) to examine how positive parental behavior in infancy may shape children's prefrontal cortex activation.

在博士論文中，我進一步驗證，六個月大嬰兒期的正向父母行為，與五歲幼兒的執行功能行為表現及前額葉皮質活化具有顯著相關，提供正向父母育兒行為對大腦可塑性影響的重要實證依據。2023 年，我以嬰幼兒早期認知發展軌跡為主題，赴芬蘭參與歐洲兒童發展與實務研討會（ECDP 2023）發表研究成果；同年，亦於馬來西亞拉曼大學（UTAR）舉辦之 IWALS 2023，發表有關親子互動與大腦發展的研究成果。

My dissertation demonstrated that positive parental behavior at six months of age is significantly associated with executive function performance and prefrontal cortex activation in five-year-olds — providing empirical evidence for how positive parenting shapes brain plasticity. In 2023, I presented findings on early cognitive-development trajectories at the European Conference on Developmental Psychology (ECDP 2023) in Finland, and shared research on parent-child interaction and brain development at IWALS 2023, hosted by Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (UTAR), Malaysia.

碩士論文則聚焦幼兒視覺空間工作記憶與翻牌遊戲表現之關聯性，研究發現兩者具顯著關聯，可作為學習區設計時納入相關遊戲、促進幼兒視覺空間工作記憶能力的參考依據；此研究亦以第一作者身分發表於 TSSCI 期刊《教育科學研究期刊》。

My master's thesis examined the relationship between preschoolers' visuospatial working memory and their performance in a card-matching game, finding a significant association — offering a practical reference for incorporating such games into learning-center design. This work was published, as first author, in the TSSCI-indexed Journal of Research in Education Sciences.

目前我持續撰寫並投稿學術論文，發表於《教育科學研究期刊》、《人類發展與家庭學報》等 TSSCI 或正面表列期刊，並參與國內外研討會發表，同時與王馨敏教授合作撰寫 Springer 出版之英文專書相關章節。此外，我亦主編並撰有多篇教科書專章，包括《親職教育》中的兩個章節，以及《嬰幼兒行為觀察與記錄》中的〈樣本描述法〉一章（2026 年出版）。

I continue to write and submit academic papers to TSSCI and other indexed journals, present at conferences in Taiwan and abroad, and collaborate with Professor Wang on a chapter for an upcoming Springer volume. I have also edited and authored several textbook chapters, including two chapters in Parenting Education and the "Specimen Description Method" chapter in Observing and Recording Infant & Toddler Behavior (published 2026).

隨著我從實務現場走向師資培育與大學課堂，我的研究視野也從親子互動，延伸至師生互動對幼兒執行功能與大腦發展的潛在影響，期望將過往親子互動的研究方法與經驗，應用於理解教育現場中師生關係的發展性意義。

As my work has moved from the classroom into teacher education and higher education, my research lens has broadened from parent-child interaction to also encompass teacher-student interaction and its potential influence on children's executive function and brain development — extending the methods and insights from my earlier work to understand the developmental significance of teacher-student relationships.
