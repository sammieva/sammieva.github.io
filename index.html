從幼兒園教室到研究實驗室From the Preschool Classroom to the Research Lab

我是周姍姍，國立臺灣師範大學幼兒與家庭科學學系專案助理教授，教育學博士。十二年來，我橫跨公私立幼兒園、特殊教育與國小附設幼兒園現場，累積豐富的教學實務與行政經驗；2023 年起於臺師大任教，並於 2026 年 8 月獲聘為專案助理教授，持續投入師資培育、課程設計與教育實習指導工作。

I am Shan-Shan Chou, a Project Assistant Professor in the Department of Human Development and Family Studies at National Taiwan Normal University (NTNU), and I hold a Ph.D. in Education. Over twelve years, I worked across public and private kindergartens, special-education settings, and elementary-affiliated preschools, building deep practical and administrative experience. I have taught at NTNU since 2023 and was appointed Project Assistant Professor in August 2026, continuing my work in teacher training, curriculum design, and student-teaching supervision.

我重視「幼兒為本」的教學設計，擅長以創意結合生活經驗與 STEM 理念，推動課程教育化、多元化與趣味化。我目前教授《幼兒教保概論》、《幼兒教育教材教法》、《幼兒教育課程設計》、《嬰幼兒行為觀察》等核心必修課程，並擔任教育實習指導教師，協助師資生從課室模擬走向幼兒園實地教學。

My teaching centers the child as the starting point for curriculum design. I enjoy weaving lived experience and STEM thinking into playful, multidisciplinary lessons. I currently teach core courses including Introduction to Early Childhood Care and Education, Materials and Methods for Early Childhood Education, Early Childhood Curriculum Design, and Infant & Toddler Behavior Observation, and I supervise student teachers moving from classroom simulation into real kindergarten practicum.

在博士攻讀期間，我加入王馨敏教授創立的「嬰幼兒大腦發展與學習實驗室」，聚焦早期親子互動品質與執行功能發展之關聯性，並運用功能性近紅外光譜（fNIRS）技術，探索嬰兒期正向父母行為對兒童前額葉活化的潛在影響。相關成果發表於《教育科學研究期刊》（TSSCI）與《人類發展與家庭學報》，並於芬蘭、馬來西亞及國內多場國際學術研討會發表。

During my doctoral studies, I joined the Infant Brain Development and Learning Lab founded by Professor Hsin-Min Wang, focusing on how early parent-child interaction quality relates to executive function development, using functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) to explore how positive parental behavior in infancy shapes children's prefrontal cortex activation. This work has been published in the Journal of Research in Education Sciences (TSSCI) and the Journal of Human Development and Family Studies, and presented at conferences in Finland, Malaysia, and across Taiwan.

教學理念核心：理論 × 實務 × 反思的師資專業建構Teaching philosophy: building professional competence through theory × practice × reflection

展望未來，我將持續結合幼教現場的實務經驗與學術研究的紮實訓練，深化教學創新與課程發展，並將研究視野從親子互動，延伸至師資培育現場中的師生互動與大腦發展，推動早期教育中互動關係與發展支持的知識應用。

Looking ahead, I aim to keep bridging hands-on early childhood practice with rigorous academic research — deepening teaching innovation and curriculum development while extending my research lens from parent-child interaction to teacher-student interaction and brain development within teacher education, so this knowledge can translate into real support for early learning.
