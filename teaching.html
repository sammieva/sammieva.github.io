教學Teaching

理論 × 實務 × 反思的師資專業建構Building Professional Competence through Theory × Practice × Reflection

自 110 學年度第 2 學期起任教於臺師大幼兒與家庭科學學系，112 學年度第 1 學期起轉任專任講師，115 學年度（2026 年 8 月）獲聘為專案助理教授，每學期授課總學分數達 14 學分。 Teaching at NTNU's Department of Human Development & Family Studies since spring 2022, full-time since fall 2023, and appointed Project Assistant Professor in August 2026, carrying 14 credit-hours per semester.

教學理念Teaching Philosophy

從「學習者中心」到「專業職能落地」From Learner-Centered Design to Professional Competence

我重視幼兒為本的教學設計，擅長以創意融合生活經驗與 STEM 理念，推動課程教育化、多元化與趣味化，進而激發幼兒學習動機與表達能力。在師資培育現場，我負責教授多門核心課程，包括《幼兒教保概論》、《幼兒教育教材教法》、《幼兒教育課程設計》、《嬰幼兒行為觀察》等必修課程，並擔任教育實習指導教師，協助學生從課室模擬到進入幼兒園實地教學，完成教學歷練與專業實踐。

I center curriculum design on the child, weaving lived experience and STEM thinking into playful, multidisciplinary lessons that spark children's motivation and expression. I teach several core courses in the teacher-education program — including Introduction to Early Childhood Care and Education, Materials and Methods for Early Childhood Education, Early Childhood Curriculum Design, and Infant & Toddler Behavior Observation — and I supervise student teachers as they move from classroom simulation into real preschool practicum.

以《幼兒教育教材教法》為例，我以 Kolb（1984）經驗學習循環為課程核心架構，結合《幼兒園教保活動課程大綱》目標與六大領域內涵，引導師資生理解並實踐學習區教學模式。學生透過探索並比較各類學習區教材教法、分析教具與素材特性、設計任務卡與鷹架工具，在接連的實作、反思、教案撰寫與模擬試教中，逐步學習如何將材料經驗轉化為具教育意圖的學習活動；課程亦強調「理論與現場結合」，學生須入園試教、執行學習區活動、蒐集觀察紀錄並進行省思修訂，讓教學能力在真實場域中獲得驗證與提升。

In Materials and Methods for Early Childhood Education, I use Kolb's (1984) experiential learning cycle as the course backbone, aligned with Taiwan's Preschool Curriculum Guidelines and its six learning domains, to guide preservice teachers through learning-center pedagogy. Students compare materials and methods across learning centers, analyze teaching aids, design task cards and scaffolding tools, and cycle through hands-on practice, reflection, lesson planning, and mock teaching. The course insists on connecting theory with the field: students teach in real preschools, run learning-center activities, collect observation records, and revise their practice based on reflection.

除了教學實務與師資輔導外，我亦關注師資生對幼教倫理與專業省思的養成。在《幼保人員專業倫理》課程中，我指導學生實地訪談幼兒園教師，探討實務工作中常見的倫理兩難與專業抉擇；學生所彙整之案例最終入選第七屆全國專業倫理個案競賽，展現其對幼教倫理議題的敏銳覺察與批判思維能力。此外，我亦強調師資生須具備「親職合作與家庭工作的能力」，在《親職教育》課程中，我帶領學生實際規劃並辦理親職講座，從前期需求調查、主題擬定、簡報製作到現場互動策略，皆由學生親自操作，讓他們在真實的家庭與教育情境中獲得面對家長的經驗與信心。

Beyond teaching practice and mentoring, I care deeply about cultivating professional ethics and reflection in preservice teachers. In Professional Ethics for Early Childhood Educators, I guide students through field interviews with practicing teachers to surface real ethical dilemmas; a case package my students compiled was selected for the 7th National Professional Ethics Case Competition. In Parenting Education, students plan and run an entire parenting workshop themselves — from needs assessment to slide design to live facilitation — building genuine confidence in working with families.
